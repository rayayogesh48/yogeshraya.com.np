# Designing the Karobar App: Simplifying Bookkeeping for Small Businesses

A shopkeeper spends the day serving customers, managing stock, and making sales. But when the day ends, there are still payments to track, expenses to record, and numbers to make sense of.

**Running a business is already hard. Keeping track of it shouldn't be.**

The Karobar app is a bookkeeping and business management app built to simplify these everyday operations for small businesses in Nepal.

As a product designer at Bytecare Technology, I work across the Karobar app's mobile and web experiences, designing workflows that help business owners manage finances, inventory, and daily operations with less friction.

## The Challenge

Small business owners don't have time to fight complicated software. They need to record transactions quickly, understand their financial position, and manage inventory without disrupting their daily work.

The challenge was to make bookkeeping more approachable without sacrificing the flexibility real businesses need.

## My Approach

I focused on the workflows that matter most to everyday business operations:

- **Onboarding:** Reduce initial setup and help users reach the product's value sooner.
- **Financial dashboard:** Make essential business figures and actions easier to find.
- **Transaction management:** Simplify recording, searching, and managing financial records.
- **Inventory management:** Support different pricing models, measurement units, and stock requirements.

Each decision started with a practical question: **How can this task become easier for the person doing it every day?**

## The Solution

Rather than treating each screen as an isolated design problem, I worked across the broader product experience, connecting onboarding, financial visibility, transactions, and inventory into more coherent workflows.

Beta testing also helped surface issues beyond the interface, including payment status updates, quotation sharing, and inconsistent web workflows. These findings informed further opportunities to improve usability and workflow reliability.

## Beyond Karobar

Karobar is part of a family of localized business management products serving five markets: Nepal, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Nigeria, and the Philippines.

Designing within this ecosystem means balancing a consistent product foundation with the languages, workflows, and business needs of different markets.

## What I Learned

Good product design isn't about adding more features or making screens look cleaner. It's about understanding how people work, identifying where they struggle, and removing unnecessary effort from the tasks they repeat.

My goal with Karobar is to make complex business operations feel simple, so business owners can spend less time managing their records and more time running their businesses.
